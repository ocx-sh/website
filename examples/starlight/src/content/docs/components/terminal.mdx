Recordings come from running the real `ocx` binary (asciicast v2, player pinned to asciinema-player 3.15.1).
The cast file is fetched as soon as the page loads; the player itself loads on first hover, focus or touch for an
open terminal, and on first expand for a collapsed one.
The player's own control bar stays off (`controls: false`); a themed play/pause button, a native seek range and a
fullscreen button replace it, wired to the player through its API (play, pause, seek, getCurrentTime, getDuration).

Select the title bar, or focus it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>, to play the recording. Closing it
pauses and rewinds.

## Events

- `ocx:collapsible:change` with detail `{ open }`, when the reader expands or collapses a collapsed terminal.

## Keyboard

- <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> expands or collapses a collapsed terminal; its title bar is a native button.
- The play button and the fullscreen button are native buttons: <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> activates
  whichever is focused.
- The seek range is a native `<input type="range">`: the arrow keys move it, which seeks the recording.

Every third-party crate in `ocx`, with its licence. Search, filter by licence, and expand a
row for its author and summary. Built on [SearchField](../search-field/), [Select](../select/)
and [Collapsible](../collapsible/) rows.

## Stories

<Stories of="dependency-explorer" />

## States

The first story's buttons show each state.

- **Loading**: skeletons and one placeholder per row to come (≤ 12); nothing moves.
- **Error**: a danger aside names the cause.
- **Empty**: no rows or no match. A known empty list reserves it from first paint, shown in the
  known-empty story.

## Events

- `ocx:collapsible:change` `{ open }` from a row.
- `ocx:deps:load` (input): reload, from `detail.data` (JSON or a promise) if given.

## Keyboard

<kbd>Tab</kbd> reaches each control and row name; <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> toggles a row;
the filter takes the Select keys.

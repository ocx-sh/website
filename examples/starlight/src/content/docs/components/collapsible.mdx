A button that shows or hides the region below it. The first paint is final; the machine loads on
the first hover or focus.

<PropsTable

The default slot is the content; the `trigger` slot replaces `label`.

## Events

- `ocx:collapsible:change` with detail `{ open }`, when the reader opens or closes the region.

## Keyboard

- <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> toggles the region; the trigger is a native button.
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves to the trigger, then into the open region.

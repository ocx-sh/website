An aside interrupts the reading flow for something the reader must not miss: a caveat, a
shortcut, a risk. For a status fact inside a sentence, use [Tag](../tag/) or
[Badge](../badges/) instead — an aside is a paragraph, not a word. [Cards](../cards/), the other
Starlight callout, moved to their own page.

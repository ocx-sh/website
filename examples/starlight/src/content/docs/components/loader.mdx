The consumer swaps the label to report progress and hides the loader once content lands. Four
layers are pulled in turn around a 2×2 grid: each lands in strong ink, settles to a quiet tone and leaves
as the next arrives. With reduced motion the grid holds still, one layer landing, and the text carries the
state. With JavaScript off it is hidden, because nothing would ever finish loading.

A stage that stays on screen while the page scrolls past it.
The items, the children of the default slot, fly in from the sides in pairs, then each is struck through, and the `finale` slot appears last.
The `head` slot holds the heading above them.
In an item, an element marked `data-stage-strike` gets the strike line, and one marked `data-stage-detail` is left out on phones, where an item is a single line.

One shared scroll listener drives it from the scroll position.
Without JS, or under reduced motion, nothing is pinned. All items show at full strength, not struck through (a struck item is dimmed), and the finale shows.
Put the stage below the first screen. It starts in that readable state and takes its place when the script runs.

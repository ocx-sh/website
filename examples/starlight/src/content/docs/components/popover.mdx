A panel of free content next to its trigger. Escape or a click outside closes it and returns
focus to the trigger. The trigger names the panel.

Slots: `trigger` (the trigger button's content) and the default slot (the panel).

## Events

- `ocx:popover:change` `{ open }`: the panel opens or closes.

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Enter, Space | On the trigger: toggle the panel. |
| Tab | Move through the panel's content; leaving it closes a non-modal panel. |
| Escape | Close the panel; focus returns to the trigger. |

A hub grid lists the entries of one ecosystem hub (`integrations` or `apps`), read from the theme's
`nav.json`: one group per category in the file's order, each entry as a card under its category.
It is the body of the `/integrations/` and `/apps/` pages.

A `planned` entry is a dashed card with a "planned" stamp and no link. An entry whose `href` leaves
the site carries the `external` icon (↗) and the accessible text "(external)". The canvases are
720px wide; open a story in a new tab to see more columns.

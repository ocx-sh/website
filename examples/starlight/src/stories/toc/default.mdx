<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

A table of contents for pages that do not have Starlight's own. Its links work before JavaScript;
the machine starts when the list scrolls into view, with no input. Scroll this story: the list
marks the heading you are reading.

<h2 id="props">Props</h2>

One entry per heading: its element id, text and level. The scroll-spy band follows the page
scroll-padding-top.

<h2 id="events">Events</h2>

`ocx:toc:change` fires when the active heading changes.

<h2 id="keyboard">Keyboard</h2>

Plain links: Tab moves between entries, Enter jumps.
